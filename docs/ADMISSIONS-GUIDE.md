# Admissions guide sources

Checked November 25, 2026. The guide uses Minerva’s public pages.
It helps families discuss roles and deadlines while the student manages
their own application. The screens are readable in any order.

| Topic | Content and source |
| --- | --- |
| Understand | [Apply](https://www.minerva.edu/apply/) offers an undergraduate entry point. [Undergraduate Admissions](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/admissions/) lists both starting routes. The [FAQ](https://www.minerva.edu/faqs/) explains the Admission Center invitation after Part 1 and the extra steps for Common Application applicants. |
| Student application | [Undergraduate Admissions](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/admissions/) describes personal and academic information, Challenges, and accomplishments. The [FAQ](https://www.minerva.edu/faqs/) describes the three parts and completing a timed challenge in one sitting. |
| School documents | The [FAQ](https://www.minerva.edu/faqs/) distinguishes the student's unofficial transcript from school verification. [Undergraduate Admissions](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/admissions/) identifies authorized senders, the delivery address for transcript services, and the separate academic form. The [counselor guide](https://www.minerva.edu/blog/application-advice-a-guide-for-minervas-transcripts-school-requirements-for-counselors-school-officials/) describes the link the student shares so the school can complete the form and send records. |
| Parent’s role | “Starting Your Application” on [Undergraduate Admissions](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/admissions/) specifies parent or guardian contact details in Part 1 for under-18 students. The [FAQ](https://www.minerva.edu/faqs/) describes signing the agreement with an under-18 student who enrolls. Tracking dates and questions is optional family support. |
| Documents | [Undergraduate Admissions](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/admissions/) lists the latest three years of academic records, possible certified translations and additional records, and examples of supporting materials for accomplishments. The [FAQ](https://www.minerva.edu/faqs/) gives final examinations and national test results as country-dependent examples. |
| What happens next? | The [FAQ](https://www.minerva.edu/faqs/) covers confirmation, Common Application completion before review, and the Enrollment Center. [Undergraduate Admissions](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/admissions/) describes academic review and publishes decision dates by round. |

## Ambiguities and scope

- The admissions page estimates 10–15 minutes for Part 1 while the FAQ
  estimates 5–10. The guide omits timing estimates.
- The admissions overview uses “submit” for the initial form. The FAQ
  also uses it for completed applications. The guide distinguishes the
  Admission Center invitation from final submission confirmation.
- The FAQ gives a confirmation window and the admissions table gives
  dates without calendar years. The guide points to the public deadlines
  and published FAQ instead of promising timing.
- Public sources describe who sends and verifies transcripts and who
  completes the academic form. The counselor guide documents the
  student-shared link. The tour stops at that published workflow and
  omits private screens and assumptions about parent access or consent.
