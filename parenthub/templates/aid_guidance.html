Translate or explain a document
  1. Keep the original intact. Work from a copy kept with your own records.
  2. Write the original title, issuing organization, dates, and currency on a separate translation sheet.
  3. Match line labels to the original page. Copy amounts as printed. Keep unfamiliar terms in the original language alongside a short explanation.
  4. Ask Minerva whether you need the full document, a certified translator, or a different format.

Fictional original excerpt

Relevé de salaire · avril 2026 · EUR

Salaire brut 2 000 · Impôt retenu 200 · Cotisation retraite 150

Illustrative English translation

Income statement, page 1 · April 2026 · EUR

Gross pay 2,000 · Income tax withheld 200 · Retirement contribution 150

Sample format · Original title and issuer · reporting period and currency · page or line · original label · English explanation · amount as printed.

For example, keep cotisation retraite beside “retirement contribution.” Ask how the plan should be described before assigning it a US category.

Practice currency conversion

Illustrative rates authored for the Parent Hub demo on November 25, 2026. Use fictional amounts. Entries stay on this page.

Currency conversion changes units. Eligibility and award amounts follow Minerva's process. The College Board says to report your home currency in the CSS Profile. Ask Minerva about its separate forms.

Example payslip · 2,000 EUR gross − 200 EUR tax − 150 EUR retirement contribution = 1,650 EUR net, assuming these are the only deductions. The original example lists the three lines separately.
